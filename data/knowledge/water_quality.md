# Water Quality Management for Tilapia

## Dissolved Oxygen (DO)
- Optimal: 5-8 mg/L
- Minimum acceptable: 4 mg/L
- Below 4 mg/L: stress, reduced feeding, mortality risk
- Aerate at dawn when DO is lowest

## Temperature
- Optimal: 27-30°C
- Tolerance: 20-35°C
- Above 32°C: disease risk increases
- Below 20°C: growth slows significantly

## pH
- Optimal: 6.5-8.5
- Below 6.0: acidic stress
- Above 9.0: ammonia toxicity risk

## Ammonia
- Total ammonia nitrogen (TAN): <0.5 mg/L
- Un-ionized ammonia (NH3): <0.02 mg/L
