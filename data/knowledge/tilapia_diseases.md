# Common Tilapia Diseases

## Streptococcosis
Caused by Streptococcus agalactiae and S. iniae. Outbreaks common above 32°C. Symptoms: erratic swimming, exophthalmia, hemorrhages. Prevention: maintain DO > 4 mg/L, avoid overcrowding, vaccinate where available.

## Columnaris (Flavobacterium columnare)
Symptoms: gray-white patches on skin/gills, frayed fins. Triggered by stress, poor water quality. Treatment: salt bath (10-15 ppt for 30 min), improve water quality.

## Parasitic Infections
Common parasites include Trichodina, Ichthyophthirius (white spot), and Dactylogyrus (gill flukes). Prevention: quarantine new stock, maintain optimal water parameters.
