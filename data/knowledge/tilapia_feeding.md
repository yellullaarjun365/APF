# Tilapia Feeding Guide

## Feed Protein Requirements
- Fry: 40-50% protein
- Fingerlings: 35-40% protein
- Grow-out: 28-32% protein
- Broodstock: 35-38% protein

## Feeding Rate
Feed 3-5% of body weight daily, divided into 2-3 feedings. Adjust based on water temperature and fish size.

## Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)
Good FCR for tilapia: 1.2-1.8. Poor FCR (>2.0) indicates overfeeding, poor water quality, or disease.
